With paediatric oncologists at [Inselspital](https://kinderklinik.insel.ch/de/), I am building continuous fever and vital-sign monitoring for children with cancer. Infections are one of the hardest parts of chemotherapy, and today we often only react once a fever has already started. We want to know whether continuous vitals can flag an infection earlier, including when a child is at home, and whether that can lighten how aggressively we have to treat.

On our side we built the path from wearable to cloud, and the interface clinicians use to follow their patients. CSEM wrote about the project in the [2024 annual report](https://www.csem.ch/en/annual-report/2024/pediatric-cancer-care/).

The first patients joined in late 2024. We are still collecting, and I cannot wait to see what we learn.
